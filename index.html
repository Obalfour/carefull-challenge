Carefull alert pipeline: results explorer

Rules flag transactions that look like known fraud patterns; an LLM reads each one next to the user's profile and history and decides whether a trusted contact should be alerted. This page shows the real output of one run on the challenge data: who gets alerted, and why.

How a transaction is judged

EnrichParse the merchant and country; an LLM extracts who receives a transfer, and spellings like "KEVIN R" / "KEVIN RICHARDS" are merged.
Flag with rulesMoney mule pass-through, amount spikes vs. the 30-day mean, and weekly overspend at new merchants. Tuned to miss nothing.
Validate with the LLMProfile, rule evidence, established behavior and related transfers go into one prompt; the model returns risk, reasoning and confidence.
DecideHigh risk alerts a trusted contact, medium means monitor, low is dismissed. Low confidence goes to a human.
